Frequently Asked Questions

How fresh is the produce?

Most products are harvested within 24-48 hours of delivery, making them significantly fresher than traditional retail options. Our "Available Now" products are ready for immediate delivery, while "Pre-Order" items will be harvested specifically for your order on the scheduled date.

How is delivery handled?

For "Available Now" products, delivery typically occurs the day after ordering. For "Pre-Order" products, delivery is scheduled for the day after the harvest date. Each farmer coordinates their own delivery to ensure the freshest possible produce reaches your doorstep.

Is there a minimum order?

There is no platform-wide minimum order, but some farmers may set minimum quantities for specific products. You can order as little or as much as you need, and even order from multiple farmers in a single transaction.

How do farmers set their prices?

Farmers set their own prices based on their costs, the quality of their produce, and market conditions. Because there are no middlemen, farmers earn more while customers often pay comparable or better prices than at retail stores for superior quality.

What if I'm not satisfied with my order?

Customer satisfaction is important to us. If you're not happy with your order, please contact the farmer directly through our platform. They are committed to resolving any issues. You can also leave reviews to help other customers make informed decisions.

How are farmers verified?

All farmers on our platform go through a verification process to ensure they meet our standards for quality and sustainability. We regularly review farmer profiles and customer feedback to maintain high standards across the platform.
